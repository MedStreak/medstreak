# Install MedStreak on your Android phone 📱

Takes about 2 minutes. It's free. You only do this once. After that the app tells you when an update is ready.

## Step 1: Download the app
1. On your phone, open this link in **Chrome**:
   **https://github.com/MedStreak/medstreak/releases/latest**
2. Scroll down to **Assets**.
3. Tap the file that ends in **.apk** (for example `MedStreak-v1.3.0.apk`).
4. If Chrome says *"This type of file can harm your device"*, tap **Download anyway** (or **OK**). Every app installed outside the Play Store shows this message.

## Step 2: Open the file
1. When the download finishes, tap **Open** on the notification at the top of your screen.
   (Can't find it? Open the **Files** app → **Downloads** → tap the MedStreak file.)

## Step 3: Allow the install (first time only)
1. Android says *"For your security, your phone isn't allowed to install unknown apps from this source."*
2. Tap **Settings**.
3. Turn on **Allow from this source**.
4. Press the **Back** button.

## Step 4: Install
1. Tap **Install**.
2. If you see **Play Protect** saying *"App blocked"* or *"Unrecognized developer"*, tap **More details** → **Install anyway**. It shows because MedStreak isn't in the Play Store, not because anything is wrong.
3. Tap **Open**.

## Step 5: Set it up
1. Tap **Allow** when MedStreak asks to send notifications. This is how your medication reminders reach you.
2. Add your first medication. Done! 🎉

## Make sure reminders arrive on time
Some phones put apps to sleep. To keep reminders working:
**Settings → Apps → MedStreak → Battery → Unrestricted** (the wording changes by phone brand).

## Updating later
When a new version is out, a card appears at the top of the **Today** screen. Tap **Update**, open the downloaded file, then tap **Install**. Your medications and streaks stay.

## Optional: turn the permission back off
After installing, you can go to **Settings → Apps → Chrome → Install unknown apps** and switch it off again.

## Something went wrong?
- **"App not installed":** delete any older copy of MedStreak first, or make sure you downloaded the newest file.
- **Download is blocked:** try again on Wi-Fi, or open the link in Chrome instead of another browser.
- **Anything else:** open an issue at https://github.com/MedStreak/medstreak/issues
